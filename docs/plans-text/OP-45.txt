Step 3: Final-Drill #40 all rivet holes in the VA-179A Choke Slide, VA-179B Choke Slide Case and VA-179C Anti-Rotation and Stop
Plate. Machine countersink the rivet hole on the outside face of the choke slide case and on one face of the anti rotation and stop
plate. Deburr the edges of all parts in this step. Remove any bow from the choke slide. Prime parts if desired.

Step 4: Install two nutplates on the VA-179B Choke Slide Case as shown in Figure 3.

Step 5: Install the four VA-179G Guides as shown in Figure 3. Trim the guides to clear the cable end ferrule holes in the VA-179B
Choke Slide Case. Install the VA-179A Choke Slide into the choke slide case. The choke slide should slide freely but not have
excess clearance to prevent vibration when assembled. Increase the heights of the openings in the choke slide case as required.

NOTE: When fully assembled with the control cables attached, the VA-179B Choke Slide Case may bow slightly depending
on the surface that the assembly is mounted too and increase the friction. Check for this problem by making a full
installation of the assembly without the VA-179C Anti-Rotation and Stop Plate installed (Step 7) and repeat Step 5 before
finishing Step 7. The springs on the carburetor alone must be able to return the choke slide to the off position.

Step 6: With both VA-179F Drilled Bolts in-place install the VA-179A Choke Slide into the VA-179B Choke Slide Case as shown in
Figure 3. The choke slide can be installed with the detent notches either way.

Step 8: Trim the length of the VA-179A Choke Slide and install the VA-104-2 Knob Black per the instructions provided with the knob
black.

Step 9: Create VA-179E Spacers similar to those shown in Figure 5 that will prevent the assembly from rocking during actuation.
Due to the fact that these spacers are custom to each installation the material has not been provided in the kit. 1/4 inch thick
spacers will provide enough clearance for the VA-104-2 Knob Black. Attach the assembly to the mount surface as shown in Figure
5.

Step 10: Attach the choke cables to the VA-179F Drilled Bolts using the VA-179D Bolt Spacers and the hardware shown in Figure 5.
Make one last check for free operation and that the springs on the carburetor will return the choke too the off position from the full
and half on positions. Check that the VA-179C Anti-Rotation and Stop Plate is installed and that there is no possibility of the back
end of the VA-179A Choke Slide from coming out of the VA-179B Choke Slide Case and jamming the assembly.
