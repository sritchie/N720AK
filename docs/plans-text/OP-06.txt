           INSTALLING THE RV-8/8A MANUAL AILERON TRIM

Step 1: Early RV-8 fuselage kits included WD-807 Control Columns which did not have the WD-807E
Spring Retainer welded in place. If your control column does not have a spring clip installed
approximately 7 ¼ inches aft of where the aileron pushrods attach, call Van’s Aircraft and request a WD-
807E Spring Clip. Install the spring clip per instructions shipped with WD-807E.
Step 2: Fabricate F-8109 Slide Tube per DWG OP-6. Use the F-8119E Connector Plate to test fit the fork
end of the slide tube.
Step 3: De-burr all holes & edges, round corners if/as desired, and prime F-8109, F-8110, F-8111, F-
8119A, F-8119B, F-8119C, and F-8119E. (Note the areas on F-8109 where DWG OP-6 calls for no paint
or primer.)
Step 4: Enlarge the SB437-4 snap bushing inside diameter from ¼ inch to 21/64 inch so as to allow the
5/16 inch diameter F-8109 slide tube to pass through. See DWG OP-6, Sect A-A. Experience at Van’s
Aircraft has shown that using a die grinder or “dremel” tool to carefully enlarge the snap bushing I.D. is
better and easier than using a drill bit. A slight amount of out-of-round to the snap bushing I.D. is
acceptable.
Step 5: Drill ONE 3/16 inch diameter hole for an AN3 bolt through F-8119D Friction Block. Locate the
hole per DWG OP-6, View D-D. The second hole will be drilled later. Use a drill press to ensure that the
hole is perpendicular to the base of the friction block.
Step 6: Attach a nutplate to F-8119C as called-out on DWG OP-6, View D-D.
Step 7: Gain access to the structure below the cabin floor by removing the front seat cushions, F-832
Front Seatback, F-879 Seat Ramp, and F-829A, B, C, & D Seat Floor/Footwell sub-assembly.
NOTE: All dimensions locating parts or features inside the aircraft are made either parallel or
perpendicular to the top of the F-814 inboard seat ribs. Measurements parallel to the top of the seat rib
reference the aft surface of F-804B. This means that distances are easily measured by laying a ruler on
the top of the seat rib and butting the end against F-804B. Distances perpendicular to the top of the seat
rib are easily measured using a tri-square referencing the top of the seat rib.
Step 8: Make cut-out in F-814-R Inboard Seat Rib per DWG OP-6, Rib Cut-Out Detail. (F-814-L cut-out is
covered in Step 10 below.) Use the AIL TRIM DJ Drill Template to mark the ribs for the cut-outs. The drill
template is designed to clamp to the inboard side of the rib with the top edge flush with the top of the rib.
The ¼ inch diameter holes near the top edge of the drill template are designed to fit over the seat rib rivet
heads. It is possible to get the drill template reversed, so you must pay close attention to the drawing
measurements. Be sure you are making your cut-out in the correct location.
Step 9: Locate F-8110 Tube Guide to F-814-R per DWG OP-6, View Looking Outboard at F-814-R Seat
Rib. Drill and cleco F-8110 to F-814-R using holes in F-8110 as drill guides. After removing chips and de-
burring holes, F-8110 may be riveted to F-814-R.
Step 10: Locate F-8111 Support Bracket to F-814-L per DWG OP-6, View Looking Outboard at F-814-L
Seat Rib. Use the AIL TRIM DJ Drill Template as a guide when drilling holes in the seat rib. The drill
template is designed to clamp to the inboard side of the rib with the top edge flush with the top of the rib.
The ¼ inch diameter holes near the top edge of the drill template are designed to fit over the seat rib rivet
heads. While you have the template in place, mark the F-814-L Inboard Seat Rib for the cut-out per
Drawing OP-1, Rib Cut-Out Detail. It is possible to get the drill template reversed, so you must pay close
attention to the drawing measurements to be sure you are drilling your holes in the correct locations. After
drilling, cleco F-8111 to F-814-L. If necessary, run your #30 drill through the seat rib and support bracket
to “clean-up” any slight hole mismatch. Remove the support bracket and rivet F-8119C to F-8111 per
DWG OP-6, Sect A-A. Make the rib cut-out marked earlier. Remove any metal chips, de-burr the holes
and cut-out, and cleco (but do not rivet) F-8111 to F-814-L.
Step 11: Install the cotter pins through F-8109 Slide Tube per DWG OP-6, Sect A-A, and Sect C-C.
Step 12: Install the modified SB437-4 Snap Bushing (ref Step 4) into F-8110 Tube Guide per DWG OP-6,
Sect A-A. Slide the non-forked end of F-8109 through both rib cut-outs and into the snap bushing. See
DWG OP-6, Sect A-A.
Step 13: Slide the F-8119D Friction Block onto the forked end of the F-8109 slide tube. Position the
friction block on the slide tube approximately as shown in DWG OP-6, Section A-A. Insert a bolt through
the single 3/16 hole in F-8119D and into one of the 3/16 holes pre-punched in F-8111 Support Bracket
clecoed to F-814-L. Clamp the friction block to the support bracket. Remove the clecos holding the

support bracket to the seat rib, remove the support bracket with the friction block still clamped to it, and
start the second 3/16 inch hole through the friction block using the remaining 3/16 hole in the support
bracket as a guide. Remove the friction block from the support bracket and finish drilling the 3/16 hole
through the friction block on a drill press.
Step 14: Attach two nutplates (for the bolts holding the friction block to the support bracket) to the
support bracket. Nutplates and rivets are called-out on DWG OP-6, View D-D. Rivet the support bracket
to the outboard side of F-814-L using the fasteners called-out on DWG OP-6, View Looking Outboard at
F-814-L Seat Rib.
Step 15: Assemble the F-8119E Connector Plate to the fork end of F-8109 per DWG OP-6, View D-D.
Insert the slide tube through the friction block, insert the end of the slide tube through the snap bushing
installed in F-8110, and attach the friction block to the support bracket using the hardware called-out in
DWG OP-6, Section A-A.
Step 16: Install the VA-158 extension springs in 2 places between the spring clip attached to WD-807
and the ends of the cotter pins installed through F-8109. See DWG OP-6, Section A-A.
Step 17: Fit the F-8119A Mount Plate and install nutplates to the left side of the cabin per DWG OP-6,
View Looking Outboard at F-8119A. Bend one end of the Push/Pull cable per DWG OP-6, Bowden Cable
Bend Detail and attach to F-8119B trim lever. Attach F-8119B Trim Lever and Push/Pull cable to F-8991A
Mount Plate per DWG OP-6, Section B-B. Install the mount plate/trim lever/cable assembly to the aircraft.
It is acceptable to slightly notch the lower edge of the F-860A armrest to allow full trim lever motion.
Step 18: Drill a hole in the shank of the AN3 bolt used to secure the push/pull cable to F-8119E
Connector Plate. The bolt is called-out on DWG OP-6, View D-D. The hole diameter and location is
defined on DWG OP-6, Cable Anchor Bolt Detail.
Step 19: Route the push/pull cable from the mounting plate to the lower tooling hole in F-805 to F-8119C
Anchor Bracket to the F-8119E Connector Plate. Secure the end of the push/pull cable to F-8119E using
the previously drilled AN3 bolt. See DWG OP-6, Isometric View and Section A-A. Trim and/or adjust the
cable so that when the trim lever is in the neutral position, the slide tube is also in the neutral position.
Step 20: Check the system for any binding or interference by running the trim lever to both extremes,
moving the ailerons through their full range of motion. Adjust the torque on the bolts attaching F-8119D so
that there is just enough friction to prevent slide tube movement when the trim system is moved to one
extreme and the control stick is moved to the other. Correct any instances of interference or binding
before proceeding.


INSTALLING THE WD-807E SPRING CLIPS
NOTE: The aileron trim system is not intended to correct an out-of-rig condition and has been designed
to have only enough roll authority to overcome imbalances in fuel or passenger and baggage loading.
Should there be any question as to whether the aircraft is properly rigged, disconnect the trim springs and
fly the aircraft when loaded as symmetrically as possible. If during flight testing an asymmetric roll
condition is noted, trim the aircraft in roll per Section 15 of the RV-8 Builder’s Manual before reconnecting
the trim springs.
Step 1: Disconnect WD-411 Front Stick and WD-412 Rear Stick from WD-807 Control Column and
carefully rest inside the airplane. Disconnect the aileron pushrods from WD-807 Control Column.
Disconnect WD-807 from the front and rear support bearings. Remove WD-807 from the aircraft to attach
WD-807E Spring Retainer.
Step 2: Locate WD-807E Spring Retainer to WD-807 Control Column per DWG OP-1, WD-807 Control
Column and Sect B-B. A centerline marked on WD-807E and on the bottom center of WD-807 will help to
get WD-807E properly oriented. Drill and cleco WD-807E to WD-807. De-burr holes. Thread one end of
each of the springs over each end of WD-807, and rivet WD-807E to WD-807. While it is possible to
remove and install springs after WD-807E is riveted in place, it is definitely easier to install springs to WD-
807E before riveting in place.
Step 3: Re-connect all the control system components that were disconnected in Step 1 above. The
control system is critical to flight safety! Double check control movements and all nuts and bolts to ensure
that nothing has been overlooked.
