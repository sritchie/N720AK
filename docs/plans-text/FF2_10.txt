Step 1: Drill four #30 pilot holes in the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle. See Figure 1.

Step 2: Place the VA-187 4" Flanged Duct on the aft face of the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle per the dimensions in Figure 1. Trace the inside of the 4" flanged duct onto the cylinder 6 aft baffle, set the duct aside and then remove the traced circular area from the cylinder 6 aft baffle (see the hatched round area in Figure 1).

Step 3: Center the VA-187 4" Flanged Duct on the hole created in Step 2. Match-Drill #30 the holes (oriented as shown in Figure 1) in the flange of the flanged duct into the aft face of the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle.

Step 4: Enlarge the two center pilot holes drilled in Step 1 to 3/4 dia. with a Unibit. Connect the quadrants as shown in Figure 1 and remove the remaining material from the hatched area of both the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle and the VA-187 4" Flanged Duct as shown in Figure 1. Remove the 4" flanged duct.

Step 5: Deburr the edges and drilled holes in both the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle and the VA-187 4" Flanged Duct.

Step 6: Rivet the VA-187 4" Flanged Duct to the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle per the callouts in Figure 1.

Step 7: Cleco the CB-1003C and CB-1004B Doubler Plates to the CB-1004A Cylinder 6 Baffle. Final-Drill #40 the rivet holes common to the doubler plates and the cylinder 6 baffle. See Figure 2.

Step 8: Dissassemble then deburr the CB-1003C and CB-1004B Doubler Plates and the CB-1004A Cylinder 6 Baffle. Dimple the rivet holes in the doubler plates and the corresponding holes in the cylinder 6 baffle.

Step 9: Rivet the CB-1003C and CB-1004B Doubler Plates to the CB-1004A Cylinder 6 Baffle (flush head on the inboard face). Rivet callouts are given in Figure 2.

Step 1: Lay out a rivet pattern on the aft side of the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle corresponding to the flange of the CB-1004A Cylinder 6 Baffle. See Figure 1 and Figure 2.

Step 2: Cleco the CB-1004A Cylinder 6 Baffle and CB-1006A Left Aft Case Baffle to the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle. See Figure 1.

Step 3: Drill #30 the rivet locations layed out in Step 1 in both the CB-1004A Cylinder 6 Baffle and the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle. Final-Drill #30 the two pre-existing holes common between the cylinder 6 baffle and the cylinder 6 aft baffle.

Step 4: Final-Drill #30 the rivet holes common between the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle and the CB-1006A Left Aft Case Baffle.

Step 5: Cleco the CB-1004C Stiffener to the CB-1004A Cylinder 6 Baffle. Final-Drill #30 the holes common between these two parts.

Step 6: Final-Drill #27 the hole in the lower flanges of the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle and CB-1006A Left Aft Case Baffle. See Figure 1.

Step 7: Dissassemble all parts drilled in Step 3, 4 and 5. Deburr the CB-1004A Cylinder 6 Baffle, CB-1004C Stiffener, CB-1006A Left Aft Case Baffle and CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle. Step 8: Rivet the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle to the CB-1004A Cylinder 6 Baffle and CB-1006A Left Aft Case Baffle. Rivet the CB-1004C Stiffener to the CB-1004A Cylinder 6 Baffle. Rivet callouts are given in Figure 1.

Step 9: Cleco the CB-1005B Oil Cooler Brace to the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle. Make the lower edge of the oil cooler brace parallel with the lower edge of the cylinder 6 aft baffle. See Figure 3.

Step 10: Match-Drill #40 the holes in the aft flange of the CB-1005B Oil Cooler Brace into the CB-1005A Cylinder 6 Aft Baffle.

Step 11: Remove the cleco from Step 9 and final-drill #12 the two holes called out in Figure 3.
