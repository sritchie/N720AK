NOTE: This section describes the installation of the landing light and the
position/strobe light in the left wing tip. Repeat this section for the right wing
tip. The right installation is a mirror of the left.

NOTE: The right side of the aircraft uses a LN A650-PG-14 Green Position Light
and Strobe and the left uses a LN A650-PR-14 Red Position Light and Strobe.
Both of these lights can be purchased separately or as part of a lighting
system from VAN'S AIRCRAFT ACCESSORY CATALOG. The most popular
lighting system is PN# LN SYS6.

Step 6: Center the W-1032 Landing Light Base Plate on the spanwise face of the wing tip as shown in Figure 4. Match-Drill #27 each of the three landing light base plate screw
holes into the wing tip. Mark the profile of the spanwise face on the aft side of the landing light base plate, remove the landing light base plate, then trim away the excess material.

Step 7: Using an MS21051-L06 Nutplate as a drill guide, match-drill #40 the two forward most sets of nutplate attach holes into the chordwise face of the W-1015-L Wing Tip. Use
a K1000-06 Nutplate as a drill guide to match-drill #40 the remaining nutplate attach holes into both faces of the wing tip. Orient the nutplates approximately as shown in Figure 4.

Step 8: Machine countersink the nutplate attach holes for the head of an AN426AD3 rivet (flush on the outside faces).

Step 1: Attach the base plate for the LN A650-PR-14 Red Position Light and Strobe to the W-1015-L Wing Tip using the hardware
called out in Figure 1.

NOTE: Leave enough slack in the wires to allow the LN A650-PR-14 Red Position Light and Strobe to be removed and the
wire connectors to be pulled through the W-1015-L Wing Tip to be disconnected.

Step 2: Connect the wires coming out of the LN A650-PR-14 Red Position Light and Strobe using the instructions provided from
Whelen (see also the instructions included with the LN SYS6 Strobe/Position Lighting System Kit). Reassemble the remaining parts
of the red position light and strobe to the baseplate.
