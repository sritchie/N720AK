  NOTE: The construction technique for the ailerons is similar to that of the rudder and elevators. The aileron uses ribs at the
  ends only while light angle stiffeners support the rest of the skin. The aileron must be kept flat while drilling and riveting. A
  cradle to hold the aileron in a vertical position while riveting is required. Since the aileron has a thinner cross section than the
  flap, make the cradle using the FL-1004-L Flap Nose Rib as shown in the flap assembly manual Page 2, Figure 1. The cradle
  need not be a perfect fit for the aileron. It is intended mainly as a support for holding the aileron in a position suitable for
  riveting.
